About me

I work as a self-employed AI trainer. On behalf of international platforms, I review and evaluate responses from AI language models and create training data, mainly in German. German is my native language and I speak English fluently. Studying Classical Archaeology at the University of Tübingen taught me to examine sources critically.

Services

  • Fact-checking responses I check responses from language models for factual errors, do my own research in sources and explain my assessment in writing.
  • Rating audio, images and video I assess AI-generated voice recordings, images and videos, for example for naturalness and whether the instructions were followed.
  • Writing tasks for AI I write instructions (prompts) with defined constraints that are used to test and train models.
